1. What We Collect

Only what is needed to play: Player ID (random local ID), game progress (valuation, security, verses, fragments, streak), leaderboard name you choose, and device type for layout. We do NOT collect real name, email, address, contacts, or precise location unless you contact us.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No chat, no strangers, no direct messages
  • No personalized ads
  • No tracking across other apps
  • Analytics OFF by default

3. Age Rating

Age 13+ / Teen. Fantasy violence (light vs darkness), no blood, no gambling with real money. In-app purchases are optional and require guardian approval for under 18.

4. Data Storage

Your save is stored locally on your device (localStorage) and in any Export file you download. If you use Export/Import, that file is yours. We do not run a live cloud account in the web preview.

5. Data Deletion — Your Right

In-Game: Settings > Privacy > Delete All My Data > Type DELETE > Confirm. This clears local save instantly.

Email: Send to privacy@christfire.game Subject: DELETE MY DATA + your Player ID. We delete within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Delete App: Deleting the app deletes your local save.

6. Data Export (GDPR / CCPA)

Email privacy@christfire.game Subject: EXPORT MY DATA + Player ID. We will send your progress data within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 shared data, email privacy@christfire.game and we will delete it.
